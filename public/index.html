Event booking made easier with Dibs!

Efficiently book limited-capacity events without the hassle of managing guestlists or the fear of double bookings. Made for small business owners, by a small business owner.

Organize your first event here! View a sample event here!

How it Works

  1. Create an event and specify capacity.
  2. Share the invitation link.
  3. Guests reserve a spot with a name and email.
  4. Once capacity reached, Dibs! no long accepts sign-ups.
